# Baseline: plays by the percentages, has not opened the math

The learner is a returning BG3 player who knows the vocabulary (advantage, saves, resistances, concentration) but has been accepting the UI percentages without knowing the formula behind them. They asked unprompted why advantage changes the number the way it does, which is the right first question. Lesson 0001 targets exactly this gap.

## Evidence
Stated directly in conversation on 2026-09-13: "there's some stuff that I kind of accept without totally understanding why, like why the percentage hit chance with advantage or disadvantage."

## Implications
- Do not re-teach what advantage *is*. Teach the arithmetic and the stacking rules, then move to saves and Spell Save DC.
- A misconception was introduced by the teacher, not the learner: high ground was described as granting advantage. bg3.wiki says it is a flat +2 / −2. Corrected in lesson 0001. Watch for it resurfacing.
- Next zone of proximal development, once lesson 0001 is confirmed: saving throws and targeting the weak save, since that is where a Sorcerer's decisions actually live.
