# Baldur's Gate 3 Mechanics Resources

## Knowledge

- [bg3.wiki: Attack roll](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Attack_roll)
  Community wiki, the most accurate source for BG3 rules. Use for: the hit formula, natural 1 and 20 rules, crit damage.
- [bg3.wiki: Advantage](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Advantage)
  Use for: how advantage and disadvantage roll, why they never stack, how they cancel, and the average-roll numbers.
- [bg3.wiki: List of sources of advantage and disadvantage on attack rolls](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/List_of_sources_of_advantage_and_disadvantage_on_attack_rolls)
  Use for: checking why a given tooltip shows advantage or disadvantage.
- [bg3.wiki: High ground rules](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/High_ground_rules)
  Use for: the +2 / -2 flat modifier and the extra range from elevation. BG3 differs from tabletop here.
- [bg3.wiki: Saving throw](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Saving_throw)
  Use for: the Spell Save DC formula and which classes are proficient in which saves.
- [bg3.wiki: Spellcasting](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Spellcasting)
  Use for: which spells roll an attack against AC and which spells allow a saving throw, and the spellcasting ability of each class.
- [bg3.wiki: Abilities](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Abilities)
  Use for: the ability score to modifier table, and the Examine shortcut (T) that shows a target's saving throws.
- [Prima Games: How to examine enemy weaknesses and resistances in Baldur's Gate 3](https://primagames.com/gaming/how-to-examine-enemy-weaknesses-and-resistances-in-baldurs-gate-3)
  Guide site, not the wiki. Use only for: what the Examine panel displays (resistances and conditions). Prefer bg3.wiki for any rule.
- [bg3.wiki: Concentration](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Concentration)
  Use for: the concentration DC formula and the full list of things that break it.
- [bg3.wiki: Karmic Dice](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Karmic_Dice)
  Use for: what the default dice setting actually does to your odds.
- [bg3.wiki: Sorcerer](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Sorcerer)
  Use for: Metamagic costs and which levels grant new options.
- [bg3.wiki: Prone (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Prone_(Condition))
  Use for: the 3 m advantage limit, the half-movement stand-up cost, the duration, and the turn-end and concentration rule.
- [bg3.wiki: Paralysed (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Paralysed_(Condition))
  Use for: the automatic critical hit within 3 m and the automatic failure of Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
- [bg3.wiki: Stunned (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Stunned_(Condition))
  Use for: the loss of actions, the automatic save failures, and the concentration break.
- [bg3.wiki: Sleeping (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Sleeping_(Condition))
  Use for: the 1.5 m critical hit rule and the full list of ways the condition ends.
- [bg3.wiki: Restrained (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Restrained_(Condition))
  Use for: the movement block, the disadvantage on its own attack rolls, and the Dexterity save penalty.
- [bg3.wiki: Enwebbed (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Enwebbed_(Condition))
  Use for: the exact effect lines of the condition that the Web spell applies.
- [bg3.wiki: Blinded (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Blinded_(Condition))
  Use for: the 3 m range reduction, the attack roll disadvantage, and the opportunity attack block.
- [bg3.wiki: Frightened (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Frightened_(Condition))
  Use for: the BG3 movement block, which differs from tabletop, and the disadvantage on ability checks.
- [bg3.wiki: Threatened (Condition)](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Threatened_(Condition))
  Use for: why a ranged attack shows disadvantage next to an enemy, and what the condition does not affect.
- [bg3.wiki: Sleep](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Sleep)
  Use for: the combined 24 hit point threshold and the immunity of Undead and Fey Ancestry creatures.
- [bg3.wiki: Grease](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Grease)
  Use for: the Dexterity saving throw, the 1 turn Prone duration, and the 10 turn surface duration.
- [bg3.wiki: Burning Hands](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Burning_Hands)
  Use for: the Dexterity saving throw and the half damage on a successful save.
- [bg3.wiki: Thunderwave](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Thunderwave)
  Use for: the Constitution saving throw, the half damage, and the push on a failed save.
- [bg3.wiki: Ray of Sickness](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Ray_of_Sickness)
  Use for: the spell that needs an attack roll first and then a Constitution saving throw against Poisoned.
- [bg3.wiki: Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Tasha%27s_Hideous_Laughter)
  Use for: the Wisdom saving throw, the Prone plus Hideous Laughter effect, and the repeated save on damage.
- [bg3.wiki: Hold Person](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Hold_Person)
  Use for: the humanoid-only restriction, the automatic hit and critical hit lines, and the end-of-turn Wisdom saving throw. Note: BG3 applies a Hold Person condition, not Paralysed.
- [bg3.wiki: Web](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Web)
  Use for: the Dexterity saving throw, the Enwebbed condition, and the concentration requirement.
- [bg3.wiki: Blindness](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Blindness)
  Use for: the Constitution saving throw, the 10 turn duration, and the absence of a concentration requirement.
- [bg3.wiki: Shove](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Shove)
  Use for: the bonus action cost, the Athletics check against passive Athletics or Acrobatics, the push distance, and the 8 m fall that knocks a creature Prone.
- [D&D 5e Basic Rules, 2018 PDF from Wizards of the Coast](https://media.wizards.com/2018/dnd/downloads/DnD_BasicRules_2018.pdf)
  Chapter 9 (Combat) and Chapter 10 (Spellcasting) are the tabletop rules BG3 is built on. Use for: the underlying logic when a BG3 tooltip is terse. Check bg3.wiki whenever BG3 might differ.
