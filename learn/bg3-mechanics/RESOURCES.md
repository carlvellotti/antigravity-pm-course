# Baldur's Gate 3 Mechanics Resources

## Knowledge

- [bg3.wiki: Attack roll](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Attack_roll)
  Community wiki, the most accurate source for BG3 rules. Use for: the hit formula, natural 1 and 20 rules, crit damage.
- [bg3.wiki: Advantage](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Advantage)
  Use for: how advantage and disadvantage roll, why they never stack, how they cancel, and the average-roll numbers.
- [bg3.wiki: List of sources of advantage and disadvantage on attack rolls](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/List_of_sources_of_advantage_and_disadvantage_on_attack_rolls)
  Use for: checking why a given tooltip shows advantage or disadvantage.
- [bg3.wiki: High ground rules](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/High_ground_rules)
  Use for: the +2 / -2 flat modifier and the extra range from elevation. BG3 differs from tabletop here.
- [bg3.wiki: Saving throw](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Saving_throw)
  Use for: the Spell Save DC formula and which classes are proficient in which saves.
- [bg3.wiki: Concentration](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Concentration)
  Use for: the concentration DC formula and the full list of things that break it.
- [bg3.wiki: Karmic Dice](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Karmic_Dice)
  Use for: what the default dice setting actually does to your odds.
- [bg3.wiki: Sorcerer](https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Sorcerer)
  Use for: Metamagic costs and which levels grant new options.
- [D&D 5e Basic Rules, 2018 PDF from Wizards of the Coast](https://media.wizards.com/2018/dnd/downloads/DnD_BasicRules_2018.pdf)
  Chapter 9 (Combat) and Chapter 10 (Spellcasting) are the tabletop rules BG3 is built on. Use for: the underlying logic when a BG3 tooltip is terse. Check bg3.wiki whenever BG3 might differ.
