How each condition ends

  • Prone. Usually after 2 turns. Grease, ice, and Throw give 1 turn. A creature that falls Prone on its own turn ends the turn at once and loses concentration. wiki
  • Paralysed. When the duration of the source ends. Sources include Ghoulish Touch, ghoul and ghast claws, and Paralysing Ray. wiki
  • Hold Person. The target rolls a Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns. The spell also needs your concentration. wiki
  • Stunned. At the end of the turn of the affected creature. wiki
  • Sleeping. On damage, on the Wet condition, on Help, or on a Shove. wiki
  • Enwebbed. After 1 turn. The Web surface applies the condition again while the creature stays in the web. wiki
  • Blinded. The duration falls at the start of each turn. Blindness lasts 10 turns, and the target rolls a Constitution saving throw at the end of each turn to remove it. wiki
  • Frightened. At the end of the turn. Only one version of Frightened is active at a time. wiki
  • Threatened. When no hostile creature stands in melee range. wiki

Shove

Shove is a bonus action. You roll an Athletics check against a DC from the passive Athletics or the passive Acrobatics of the target, whichever is higher. Shove pushes the target 1 m to 6 m. The distance depends on your Strength and on the weight of the target. wiki

Warning: Shove does not knock a creature Prone by itself. A creature that falls at least 8 m is knocked Prone with no saving throw. A fall of 4 m or more also causes falling damage. wiki

The combination rules

  • Apply the condition first, then attack. The attack of an ally later in the turn order gets the benefit.
  • Prone and Paralysed reward melee range. Move the melee ally to within 3 m before the attack.
  • Advantage never stacks. One condition that gives advantage is enough. Add high ground for a flat +2 on top. See the d20 cheat sheet.
  • A flat 65 percent hit chance becomes 88 percent with advantage. See Lesson 1.
  • Damage ends the Sleeping condition. Do not open with an area damage spell on a sleeping group.
  • Choose the condition by the weakest saving throw of the target, not by the damage of the spell.
