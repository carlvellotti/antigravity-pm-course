Spell Save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + spellcasting ability modifier

The Sorcerer casts with Charisma. The Wizard casts with Intelligence. A natural 20 on an attack roll always hits and is a critical hit. A natural 1 always misses. A natural 1 on a concentration saving throw almost always ends the concentration.

Advantage rules

  • Advantage rolls two dice and keeps the higher die. Disadvantage rolls two dice and keeps the lower die.
  • Multiple sources of the same kind do not stack. The game still rolls two dice.
  • Any advantage plus any disadvantage cancels to one flat die.
  • High ground gives +2 flat and low ground gives −2 flat. This bonus is not advantage, and it stacks with real advantage. wiki
  • Common sources of advantage on attacks: the target is Prone, Restrained, Blinded, Paralysed or Sleeping; the attacker is Hidden or Invisible.
  • Common sources of disadvantage: a ranged attack with an adjacent enemy; a ranged attack within 3 m; the attacker cannot see the target.

Karmic Dice

The game enables Karmic Dice by default. After failed rolls it increases the chance that your next roll succeeds. It also moves damage rolls toward the average value. It applies to enemies also, but it never forces an enemy saving throw to succeed. To make the table above correct, turn Karmic Dice off in the Gameplay options. wiki
