Spell Save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + spellcasting ability modifier

Sorcerer casts with Charisma. Wizard casts with Intelligence. Natural 20 on an attack always hits and crits. Natural 1 always misses. A natural 1 on a concentration save almost always breaks it.

Advantage rules

  • Advantage: roll two, keep higher. Disadvantage: roll two, keep lower.
  • Multiple sources of the same kind do not stack. Still two dice.
  • Any advantage plus any disadvantage cancels to one flat die.
  • High ground is +2 flat, low ground −2 flat, not advantage. It stacks with real advantage. wiki
  • Common advantage on attacks: target Prone, Restrained, Blinded, Paralysed, Sleeping; attacker Hidden or Invisible.
  • Common disadvantage: ranged attack while an enemy is adjacent, ranged attack at a target within 3 m, attacker cannot see the target.

Karmic Dice

On by default. After failed rolls it raises the chance your next roll auto-succeeds, and it squashes damage rolls toward the average. It applies to enemies too, but never forces an enemy save to succeed. Turn it off in Gameplay options if you want the table above to be the truth. wiki
