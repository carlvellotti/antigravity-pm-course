The three modifiers

resistance → damage ÷ 2, rounded down vulnerability → damage × 2 immunity → damage = 0
RuleResult
Two resistances to the same typeHalf damage. The modifiers do not stack.
Resistance plus vulnerability, same typeNormal damage. The modifiers cancel each other.
Immunity plus vulnerability, same type0 damage. Immunity ignores the other modifiers.
RoundingHalve the damage first, then round down.

Sources: bg3.wiki, Damage types and bg3.wiki, Resistances.

The Examine panel

Hover the cursor over a creature and press T. Default keybinding "Examine / Pin Tooltip": bg3.wiki, Options.

Mark on the damage type iconMeaning
Grey upward arrowResistance to non-magical sources. Damage from those sources is 50% lower.
Blue upward arrowResistance to magical sources. Damage from those sources is 50% lower.
Grey and blue upward arrowResistance to both magical and non-magical sources.
Red downward arrowVulnerability. Damage of that type is 100% higher.
Yellow outline on the shieldImmunity to magical and non-magical sources.
Grey outline on the shieldImmunity to non-magical sources only.

Spells are magical sources of damage. Weapons of uncommon rarity and above are also magical sources. A spellcaster therefore reads the blue arrows. Source: bg3.wiki, Resistances.

Wet

Effect of WetResult
Lightning damageVulnerable. The damage is doubled.
Cold damageVulnerable. The damage is doubled.
Fire damageResistant. The damage is halved.
Burning and Wild Magic: BurningImmune.
Target already resists Lightning or ColdThe resistance is negated. The target does not become vulnerable.
Target is already ChilledBoth conditions are replaced with Frozen.
Target is invisibleWet ends normal invisibility immediately.

Sources of Wet: deep water, steam clouds, and the spell Create Water. Source: bg3.wiki, Wet.

Warning: Wet applies to your own party in the same way. Keep your melee characters out of the water.
