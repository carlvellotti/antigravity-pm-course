Reaction rules

  • You hold 1 reaction per turn. It recharges at the start of your turn. wiki
  • A reaction triggers on your own turn or on the turn of another creature. wiki
  • Opportunity Attack triggers when an enemy moves out of your reach. The Disengage action prevents it. wiki
  • Shield costs a reaction and a level 1 spell slot. It gives +5 Armour Class and immunity to Magic Missile until the start of your next turn. wiki
  • Some class features use the reaction system without the Reaction resource. Examples: Divine Smite, Sneak Attack, Reckless Attack. wiki
  • The Reactions tab sits on the spellbook page. The default hotkey is L. Each reaction has an enabled checkbox and an "Ask" setting for a prompt. wiki
  • A creature with the Prone, Incapacitated or Sleeping condition cannot make an opportunity attack. wiki

Concentration

Concentration DC = half the damage taken, or 10, whichever is higher
Concentration save = d20 + Constitution modifier + proficiency bonus if proficient

You hold one concentration spell at a time. A second concentration spell ends the first one. A natural 1 almost always ends the concentration. Source: bg3.wiki, Concentration.

Damage up to 20 gives DC 10, because half of 20 is 10. A natural 1 always fails and a natural 20 always succeeds, so no chance is 100% or 0%.

Events that end concentration with no saving throw

  • You cast a second concentration spell.
  • You receive the Downed, Banished, Sleeping, Incapacitated, Prone or Rage condition.
  • You dismiss the companion who holds the spell.
  • You press the Flee Combat button.
  • You cancel the spell with the x on the spell icon.

Source: bg3.wiki, Concentration. A move away from the enemies until combat ends does not break concentration.

Save proficiency and concentration

Each class gives proficiency in two saving throws. The Sorcerer is proficient in Constitution and Charisma, so the Sorcerer adds the proficiency bonus to every concentration saving throw. The Wizard is proficient in Intelligence and Wisdom, so Gale does not. Source: bg3.wiki, Saving throw.

Give the concentration spell to the character with the higher concentration save bonus.

Quickened Spell

Quickened Spell costs 3 sorcery points. The effect: "Spells that take an Action take a Bonus Action instead." The option becomes available at Sorcerer level 3. Source: bg3.wiki, Metamagic. The wiki page states no limit on two levelled spells in one turn. Test this point in the game before you plan a turn around it.
