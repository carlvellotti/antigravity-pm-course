# Mission: Baldur's Gate 3 combat mechanics

## Why
Play the current Sorcerer run with real understanding instead of accepting numbers on faith. When a hit chance says 65%, know exactly where that came from, and know which spell, save, and element to pick against a given enemy before the turn starts.

## Success looks like
- Read any hit-chance tooltip and rebuild the number by hand from d20 plus modifiers versus AC.
- Predict, before casting, whether a save-based spell is likely to land by matching it to the target's weakest save.
- Explain why advantage or disadvantage changed a percentage, and know which sources stack and which do not.
- Pick a damage type that the enemy is not resistant to, and set up at least one surface combo on purpose.
- Manage concentration deliberately: know what breaks it and keep one spell up through a fight.

## Constraints
- Learning happens between play sessions, so lessons must be short enough to finish in 10 minutes.
- Playing a Sorcerer with Gale as an Evocation Wizard. Lessons should use those two as the running examples.
- Prefers understanding the "why" over memorising build guides.

## Out of scope
- Optimal builds, multiclass dips, and item farming routes.
- Story, romance, and dialogue choices.
- Tabletop 5e rules where they differ from BG3. BG3 is the source of truth here.
